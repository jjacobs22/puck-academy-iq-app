Module 5: Forechecking

Forechecking Is About Layers

A good forecheck isn't three guys chasing the puck. It's three layers of pressure — F1 dictates, F2 supports, F3 protects. Everyone has a job.

F1's Job: Dictate, Don't Chase

F1 doesn't just charge at the puck. You take an angle that limits where the D can go — force them one way, take away their best option.

Read Before You Commit

Don't just fly in. Read if you have support before you commit to pressure. If F2 isn't there, contain. If F2 is in position, attack.
