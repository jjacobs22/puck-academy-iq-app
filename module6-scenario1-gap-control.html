Module 6: D-Zone (Defensemen)

Defense Starts With Position

As a D-man, you're the last line before the goalie. Your job isn't to make highlight hits — it's to take away time and space, make the right first play, and keep the puck out of the net.

Gap Is Everything

Too much gap? They walk around you. Too tight? They blow by you. The right gap depends on speed, support, and where you are on the ice.

First Play, Best Play

When you get the puck, you need to know your options before it arrives. Quick, smart decisions start the breakout — hesitation leads to turnovers.
