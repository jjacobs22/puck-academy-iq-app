Module 4: Offensive Zone

Offense Is About Options

In the O-zone, your job isn't just to score — it's to create problems for the defense. Be in a spot where they have to make a choice.

Hard Areas vs. Soft Areas

The net front and slot are "hard areas" — high traffic, high reward. The perimeter is "soft" — safe but less dangerous. Know when to be where.

Think One Pass Ahead

Great offensive players don't just react — they anticipate. Where's the puck going next? Be there before the defense figures it out.
